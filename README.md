# AERODROME

The engineer's sheets: the airships of Charles A. A. Dellschau (1830-1923) and his "Aero Club of Sonora", read closely. A public work in progress, by User1 (silberpunkt) and Poetas.

**[Open the sheets in your browser →](https://silberpunkt.github.io/aerodrome/)**

For each craft: Dellschau's own drawing, colour-corrected; an interpreted illustration of how it was meant to work; and a transcription of his handwriting, 111 years old, gentlemanly and enthusiastic.

- `engineer.html`: the sheets. The whole exhibit is this one page.
- `assets/__paperprints/`: the colour-corrected drawings the sheets show.
- `fonts/`: [Eniee](https://github.com/silberpunkt/eniee) Slender, OCR-B drawn again with a flat nib.

Dellschau's drawings are in the public domain. The scans came by way of Trivium Art History. Eniee is free to use without limitation.
