AERO CLUB OF SONORA  ·  ENGINEER'S SHEETS  ·  1904  ·  none of these are held up by gas
The Sonora Airostant Club.Illustrations by Chas. Dellschau, 1904-1912.
SHEETTEXTORIGINAL
This exhibit is intended to take Charles at the word of his pen. Some feel Dellschau's illustrations are intended to represent helicopters or inflatable balloons. He seemed to feel differently, and so wrote and illustrated. Actual men and women of that place and time, some well-known, are mentioned among the Club in his drawings. Joe Cheney's decalift SATURNUS, Honoré Laurence's GONDLEUR. He appeared to draw inspiration from them as a group.
